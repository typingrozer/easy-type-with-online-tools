Helpful Tips for Better Telugu Typing

Type the way you speak. Don't worry about perfect spelling in English — the tool is built to understand how Telugu words sound, including common dialect variations.

Use spaces to confirm words. The conversion happens when you press the spacebar. If a word doesn't look right, backspace to the English word, adjust the spelling, and press space again.

Practice with short sentences first. Start with greetings, names, and daily-use phrases. Within a few days, typing full paragraphs will feel natural.

Keep a list of tricky words. Some names and rare words may need a second spelling attempt. Note down what worked so next time is instant.
